# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Discord Bot ("Bot") collects, uses, processes, and protects your personal data. This policy applies to all users of the Bot, including those in the European Union and California.

## 2. Data We Collect

We collect the following data when you use the Bot:

- **Discord User IDs:** Unique identifier assigned by Discord
- **Server/Guild IDs:** Identifier for the Discord server
- **Message Content:** The text of messages processed by the Bot
- **User Profile Data:** Usernames, nicknames, avatar URLs
- **User Roles:** Your roles within the Discord server
- **Chat Logs:** Historical message data for functionality
- **Timestamps:** When messages were sent

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion

All collected data is automatically deleted after 30 days. We do not retain personal data beyond this period. Data is deleted permanently and cannot be recovered by users or the operator.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR & CCPA)

### For EU Users (GDPR)

We process your data based on the following legal grounds:

- **Consent:** By using the Bot, you consent to data processing as described herein
- **Legitimate Interest:** We have a legitimate interest in providing and improving the Bot's functionality
- **Contract Performance:** Processing is necessary to perform our obligations to you

### For California Users (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to:

- Know what personal data is collected
- Delete personal data collected (subject to exceptions)
- Opt-out of future data collection (by removing the Bot from your server)

## 5. How We Use Data

We use collected data for:

- Providing Bot functionality and features
- Responding to user commands and requests
- Storing conversation history (30-day window)
- Debugging and improving Bot performance
- Complying with legal obligations
- Detecting and preventing abuse or unauthorized use

## 6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. The Bot operates through Discord's API and Discord may have access to data in accordance with their Privacy Policy. You should review Discord's Privacy Policy as well.

## 7. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.
